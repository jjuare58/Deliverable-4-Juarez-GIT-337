L6 · Composition Plan

Story: Losing the Dark: Why Our Night Sky Is Disappearing. One row for every positioned, layered, clipped, rounded, or shape-wrapped element.

Layer scale (tokens in styles.css): --layer-base: 0, --layer-raised: 10 (pull quote), --layer-sticky: 20 (story guide), --layer-overlay: 30 (reserved, not used).

Element Content purpose Normal-flow baseline Chosen technique and why Containing block / stacking context Fallback or disengagement Required test
Badge (.issue-badge, "Science feature") Labels the story type at a glance. A small label that appears after the intro inside the header. On wide screens, position: absolute inside the header pins it to the top-right corner without moving other content. pointer-events: none so it can never block a click. Containing block: .feature-header (position: relative). No new stacking context (no z-index). Below 52rem it goes back to normal flow under the intro, because at 320px it covered the eyebrow text. 320 / 768 / 1280px; long title; 200% zoom; badge never covers the title or eyebrow.
Story guide (.story-guide) Lets readers jump to any of the 4 story sections. A box of links that comes before the article. On wide screens, position: sticky with inset-block-start: 1rem keeps it on screen while reading. Uses --layer-sticky (20). Bounded by its parent, main (the grid). Creates a stacking context (sticky + z-index). Below 52rem it stays in normal flow above the article. Sticky behavior at page and section boundaries; keyboard Tab through links; links never hidden.
Pull quote (.pull-quote) Highlights a key quote from a NOAA scientist. A normal blockquote between paragraphs. On wide screens, position: relative plus a -1.5rem margin lets it stick out past the column edge into the grid gap. Solid background and --layer-raised (10) keep it clean. Containing block: its section. Creates a stacking context (relative + z-index), which sits below the sticky guide (20). Below 52rem no negative margin, so it sits fully inside the column. Doubled paragraph; 200% zoom; check the sticky guide never covers the quote text.
Figure box (.feature-figure) Frames the Earth-at-night photo and its caption. A figure with image and caption. border-radius (Lunaris --radius-md) for softer, consistent corners. Normal flow. No new stacking context. If unsupported, square corners. Still complete. Visual check at all 3 widths.
Earth photo (.feature-figure img) Shows city lights across the Americas at night. A plain rectangular image. clip-path: polygon() gives slanted, editorial edges. Only dark space in the corners is trimmed. Inside the figure. clip-path creates its own stacking context. Inside @supports. Unsupported or disabled: a full rectangle, nothing lost. Disable clip-path in DevTools; confirm nothing important was cut.
Milky Way photo (.shape-portrait) Shows the dark sky people are losing, beside the wildlife section. A round image (border-radius: 50%) above the text. On wide screens, float + shape-outside: circle(50%) + shape-margin: 1rem wraps the text around the circle. Containing block: its section (display: flow-root keeps the float inside). No new stacking context. Inside @supports and the 52rem media query. Otherwise the round photo stacks above the text. Disable shape-outside in DevTools; check 320px and 200% zoom; text stays readable.
